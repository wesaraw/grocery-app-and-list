Balsamic Chicken Wrap with Goat Cheese, Cranberries & Lemony Arugula

30 minutes | 4 servings

Find cookware

  • citrus juicer (optional)
  • colander
  • cutting board
  • measuring cups
  • measuring spoons

Cook & enjoy

  • 1
    Wash and dry the fresh produce. (Skip the arugula if it came pre-washed.)
    1 lemon
1 bunch radish
2 (5 oz) pkgs baby arugula
  • 2
    Juice lemon into a large bowl; add oil, Dijon, maple syrup, salt, and pepper. Whisk to combine the dressing and set aside.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
1 tsp Dijon mustard
½ tsp pure maple syrup
½ tsp salt
⅛ tsp black pepper
  • 3
    Preheat a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  • 4
    While the skillet heats up, pat chicken dry with paper towels and cut into bite-sized pieces; season with salt and pepper.
    1 ½ lb chicken breasts, boneless skinless
½ tsp salt
½ tsp black pepper
  • 5
    Once the skillet is hot, add oil and swirl to coat the bottom. Add chicken and sauté, stirring occasionally, until cooked through, 4-5 minutes.
    4 tsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 6
    Meanwhile, crumble goat cheese into a small bowl and add cranberries; stir to combine and set aside.
    1 (4 oz) log goat cheese
¼ cup dried cranberries
  • 7
    Using a clean cutting board, peel, halve, and slice onion into thin half-moons.
    1 medium red onion
  • 8
    When the chicken is done, use a slotted spoon to transfer it to a plate (leaving cooking juices in the skillet).
  • 9
    Return skillet to medium-high heat. Add the onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until softened, about 4 minutes.
  • 10
    While the onion is cooking, trim off and discard tops and root ends of radishes; cut into matchsticks and add to the bowl with the dressing.
  • 11
    Add vinegar and sugar to the skillet with the onion; continue to cook, stirring constantly, until the vinegar is reduced, 1-2 minutes. Return chicken to the skillet (along with any accumulated juices), stir to combine, and remove from heat.
    ¼ cup balsamic vinegar
1 tsp brown sugar
  • 12
    Add arugula to the bowl with the dressing and radish; toss to combine.
  • 13
    Place tortillas on a flat surface and divide balsamic chicken between them; top with a small amount of salad and sprinkle with the goat cheese-cranberry mixture. Roll tightly to close and cut in half. 
    4 large flour tortillas
  • 14
    Divide wraps and remaining salad between plates. Enjoy!
